02 / Research philosophy

Keep the evidence.
Keep the uncertainty.

A useful research archive shows both what was discovered and what remains unknown. Future versions will include citations, datasets, methods, revision history, and stable paper links whenever possible.

01
Question-driven

Start with a specific, meaningful question—not a predetermined conclusion.

02
Transparent methodology

Document assumptions, sources, limitations, and work others could reproduce.

03
Preserve the process

Keep a clear distinction between completed research, work in progress, and archived attempts.
