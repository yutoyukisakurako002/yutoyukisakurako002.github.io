Verification needed: The research notes contain an ambiguous 2020 manufacturing value-added entry (“109.546.2”), interpreted here as 109,546.2 billion yen because it matches the reported productivity calculation. Confirm the value against e-Stat before publishing this as independently verified data.

Correlation was calculated using robot density and the paper's rounded productivity index: r ≈ 0.6072, two-sided p ≈ 0.1482 (n = 7). The unrounded derived productivity index yields a slightly different p ≈ 0.1473. Small sample size, uneven year intervals, common trends, and potential confounders limit inference.
